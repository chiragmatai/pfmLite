Scoring formulas
Absolute — lower: (P95 − value) / (P95 − P5)  Absolute — higher: (value − P5) / (P95 − P5)

Relative — lower: norm ÷ (P95 − P25)/(P95 − P5)  Relative — higher: norm ÷ (P75 − P5)/(P95 − P5)

Both use seeded P5/P25/P75/P95 from the DB per cohort. Absolute normalises against the full P5–P95 range. Relative divides by what a P25/P75 peer scores on the same scale.
